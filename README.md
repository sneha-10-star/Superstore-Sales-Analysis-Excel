---
Project Overview
This project involves a comprehensive analysis of retail sales data to track business performance across different regions and product categories. I transformed a raw dataset of 9,500 transactions into a functional business dashboard using Excel to provide a clear view of revenue trends.
---
📊 Business Metrics (KPIs)
Total Revenue: $2.26 Million – The total gross sales generated.

Total Orders: 9,500 – The total volume of customer transactions processed.

Average Ticket (AOV): $230.77 – The average spend per order, identifying customer value.
---
🛠️ Technical Process
---
Data Sourcing: Raw dataset sourced from Kaggle.

Data Processing: Utilized Pivot Tables to structure the analysis layer and calculate metrics.

Metric Calculation: Created custom views for Order Count and Average Sale Value to go beyond basic sum totals.

Visualizations: Built a Monthly Sales Trend line chart, Product Performance bar chart, and Regional Sales column chart.
---
📈 Key Insights
---
Top Performing Region: The West region is the leading revenue generator.

Category Leader: Technology is the primary driver of revenue compared to Office Supplies and Furniture.

Seasonality: A significant sales peak is observed in November, suggesting a strong end-of-year shopping trend.
